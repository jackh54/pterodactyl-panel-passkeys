Security

Phishing-resistant by design

A passkey only works on your panel's own address, and the private key never reaches the panel.

Strict origin check

Only responses made for your panel's exact APP_URL are accepted. A look-alike site gets nothing it can use.

Single-use challenges

Every sign-in gets a fresh random challenge that works once and expires after 5 minutes.

Clone detection

The signature counter is checked on every sign-in to catch a copied authenticator.

Private by design

A random user handle, never your email or ID. Attestation is none, so no device data is collected.

Same lockout rules

Failed passkey attempts count toward the panel's normal login lockout. Adding or removing one needs your password.

You'll always know

The owner gets an email when a passkey is added or removed, and every action lands in the activity log.
